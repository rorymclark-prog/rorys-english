# Saved practice and engagement

Only learner originals count: deduplicated saved conversations, non-audio uploads, submitted answers/revisions, replies and quiz attempts. Teacher records and software checks are excluded. The audio attachment of a saved chat does not add another performance. Saved conversation minutes are an estimate from caption timestamps, including the partner and pauses; this is neither learner talk time nor time online. Unknown timing stays excluded and explicit. Historical offline work, page visits and unsent drafts are not measured.

It resets on Monday, pauses during holidays and is reduced for short teaching weeks, with no catch-up debt or retrospective targets. Verified Vienna 2026–27 school holidays and learner calendar breaks apply. School-specific exceptions should remain in the existing calendar. Future school-year holiday/semester dates need verification before extending targets.
