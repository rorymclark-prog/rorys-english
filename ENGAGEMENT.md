# Saved practice and engagement

20 October 2026: Student Today and teacher Learning dashboards include day, week, month, Vienna semester and school-year activity history, plus three practice rings at week, unit, shared lesson and course level.

## Goals and scope

Rory authorised optional historical catch-up and confirmed the per-unit targets: 30 estimated saved conversation minutes, one writing attempt/revision and two different vocabulary items (each with a meaning and original sentence). A course adds capped per-unit contributions: 14 units for Ferdi’s current English in Context 5 course and eight provisional outline units for Valentin’s way2go! 8. Future unpublished units remain “coming later”; rings are practice foundations, not proof of full textbook coverage or mastery. Last year’s published archives remain a separate “Earlier saved units” course view rather than being mixed into this year. Shared lesson-practice rings use that lesson’s existing speaking duration, one writing attempt and two vocabulary items.

Weekly goals are 30 minutes, one writing attempt and two vocabulary items in a full teaching week. Known school/calendar breaks pause goals; short weeks reduce minutes and round vocabulary up (one writing attempt minimum). Earlier weeks can now be completed later as optional catch-up. This supersedes the previous current-week-only/no-retrospective-target rule. Holiday practice remains optional and course/unit work can be revisited without creating compulsory holiday debt. Historic calendar exceptions are limited to the saved calendar and verified holiday ranges; no extra historic holiday dates are invented.

## Evidence and catch-up

`src/lib/practice-rings.ts` derives history from existing authenticated learner originals: deduplicated timed saved conversations, real typed practice answers, named/static writing-task submissions, genuine writing document analyses and complete structured vocabulary entries. Clearly identified learner writing uploaded by Rory on their behalf also counts; raw teacher uploads without a learner-writing analysis do not. Private reviews, teacher preparation, generic quizzes, audio copies, software tests, future-dated work, task instructions and exercise worksheets do not become writing or vocabulary credit. Legacy speaking titles match a unit only where explicit; ambiguous “My current unit” conversations remain available for the learner to link. Prepared lessons do not themselves earn credit. Unknown audio timing remains excluded.

“Use saved work for these rings” appends a reference to an existing original using the authenticated learning-reply endpoint. The latest valid link moves an original’s week/unit/lesson attribution, never its original dates, bytes or answers. Each original counts once within a view; a week, lesson, unit and course can all show its appropriate contribution. Moving weekly credit to catch up an earlier week removes it from the original week’s ring total. The activity chart always keeps the true submission dates. Vocabulary is deduplicated by normalised word within each unit/week/lesson. Course contributions are capped within each unit, so surplus practice in one cannot close the entire course.

New writing and vocabulary can be saved against the selected scope through the same existing reply service. Exact original learner text is retained; the feedback view presents readable receipts rather than storage identifiers. Stable content-based IDs retain retry safety. New practice is stored against an existing shared ready lesson-practice record; unavailable/private plans are never opened to students. Device drafts are scoped to student and ring selection, survive refresh/focus and clear only after confirmed receipt. Photographs must already be saved and confirmed as the learner’s own writing; worksheet analyses are not eligible as essays.

Archived-unit speaking opens with the selected learner’s published unit context, validated on the server, and saves that named unit and course year in the conversation title. Future/unpublished and other learners’ unit IDs are rejected. Existing live-voice allowance and provider/spending settings are unchanged; completing an earlier week uses the usual allowance unless Rory separately grants more. After a conversation for an older week, students return to its rings and link the saved conversation. Past schoolwork/photos can also be linked without a new upload.

## Celebration and privacy

All three completed rings get a brief confetti burst, small ring pulse and congratulatory message. The effect is once per student/scope on that device, with no sound. Reduced motion suppresses moving effects, while the completion message remains. Teacher mode and read-only student preview neither celebrate nor write celebration/draft state. Existing authentication, private sheets, formal marks, original files and five-hour feedback rules remain unchanged. No new Apps Script deployment or tracking service is required.

Rings measure saved effort, never marks or independent mastery. The dated skill progress/assessment panels remain separate. No learner submission was fabricated to test the release.

Validation: synthetic tests cover historical backfill, late receipts, credit movement, source deduplication, capped course roll-up, private/foreign/future/unpublished exclusions, holiday/short-week handling, readable receipts, archived-unit voice selection, once-only celebration and reduced motion. The actual component was tested locally with synthetic data for partial-to-complete celebration, course totals, 390px layout and draft refresh recovery. Production verification is recorded separately in the release handoff. Existing dependency-audit findings remain outside this feature’s unchanged dependency set.
