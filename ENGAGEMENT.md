# Saved practice and engagement

20 October 2026: Teacher learning dashboards and student Today pages now show calendar-day, Monday–Sunday week, month, Vienna semester and school-year views, a saved-action chart, and three concentric weekly practice rings: speaking, writing and vocabulary.

Only learner originals count: deduplicated saved conversations, non-audio uploads, submitted answers/revisions, replies and quiz attempts. Teacher records and software checks are excluded. The audio attachment of a saved chat does not add another performance. Saved conversation minutes are an estimate from caption timestamps, including the partner and pauses; this is neither learner talk time nor time online. Unknown timing stays excluded and explicit. Historical offline work, page visits and unsent drafts are not measured.

The existing 30-minute speaking goal applies to the two current learners from the first full week after the 17 October homework reset. It resets on Monday, pauses during holidays and is reduced for short teaching weeks, with no catch-up debt or retrospective targets. Verified Vienna 2026–27 school holidays and learner calendar breaks apply. School-specific exceptions should remain in the existing calendar. Future school-year holiday/semester dates need verification before extending targets.

The full-week goals are 30 estimated saved speaking minutes, one saved writing attempt or revision, and two different vocabulary items with a meaning and original sentence. These are part of the existing homework, not additional tasks. The writing ring accepts a student answer to a valid shared ready lesson-practice task, an explicitly named writing-task submission, or the student explicitly linking their own current-week writing upload. A bare upload, teacher document, task instructions, vocabulary worksheet or quiz does not automatically close writing. Vocabulary has its own short save form; it does not infer word use or mastery from quiz scores. Same-word retries count once. Both new rings reset on Monday, use the same verified calendar and no retrospective targets, and pause during breaks. One writing attempt remains the minimum for a short active week; vocabulary is rounded up after prorating the two-word target.

New practice uses authenticated existing learning replies attached to the current shared ready task, with stable content-based receipt IDs for retries. It retains originals and teacher-feedback release rules. Student preview is read-only. Teachers can see saved vocabulary and ring totals in the existing engagement panel; full writing remains linked to its original task/document. Completion acknowledges saved effort, never accuracy, marks or independent mastery.

All reads retain existing authentication and student-preview privacy rules; no backend deployment, new tracking service or permission change is required. Source errors do not become zero activity. The existing skill progress/assessment panels remain separate.

Validation: engagement tests cover deduplication, learner/teacher separation, date boundaries, holidays, partial weeks, missing timing and ring resets, valid practice receipts, duplicate/incomplete vocabulary, own-document links and retry identifiers; full repository tests, type checks and production build pass. Existing dependency-audit findings are outside this feature's unchanged dependency set.

The two failed document readings were repaired in their original private progress sheets; original files and dates, teacher-feedback fields and release rules are preserved. Private progress summaries remain assistant drafts pending Rory review. These private records are not included in this repository.
